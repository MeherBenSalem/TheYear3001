# Patch notes

## 2.0.0 — Impact

The mod is now **Chronofall** (mod id `chronofall`). Chrono Rifts open in the night sky and Chrono Pods crash down with
a Rift Cache and Sentinels that fire laser bolts; the Fabricator prints tech with a hologram; the Flux Charger powers
everything without cables; and you build a jetpack, a drone with chips, a rideable mech with a ground slam, a hover
skiff, a plasma cutter, a pulse rifle and rift gates. Old Robotica and The Year 3001 worlds load with their blocks,
items and robots migrated. Ores generate again, recipes and drops work on every version, oil is gone. Available for
Minecraft 1.20.1 (Fabric, Forge), 1.21.1, 26.1.2, 26.2 and 26.3 (Fabric, NeoForge).

Full notes: [patchnotes/2.0.0.md](patchnotes/2.0.0.md).
